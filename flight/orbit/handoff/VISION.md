# The register: what this is meant to feel like

From the owner, 2026-11-21, for the Unreal version. It records where the idea comes from and the tone it
should have. It is not a script: the story and its people are the game's own (the canon's Residents and
the Director, `canon/ARCHITECTURE.md` §3.6 to §3.10), and nothing is lifted from the films below.

## The three films it grew from

- **Interstellar.** Spaceflight treated as physics, not as magic, and the feelings that its scale gives:
  - a runway that becomes orbit;
  - an Earth seen whole;
  - a docking against a turning station that has to be got exactly right.
  
  The sketch already works this way: the core is physics with no cheats, and the docking matches the port's
  spin.
- **Elysium.** A wheel in orbit with a town on its floor, an orbit above an Earth that has its own life,
  and shuttles that come in turning with the wheel and settle onto it. The flight deck's landing is this:
  co-rotating, tracking the wheel's curve like an airliner on a curved glide path, then a hover onto a
  pad. `OPEN_RING_AIR.md` has the owner's two ways to give that deck air.
- **Total Recall (2012).** An ordinary person in a dead-end job, building robots on a welding line, who
  finds they are more than they thought: an identity hidden from them, a partner who is not who she
  seemed, a climb from nobody to someone the world turns on. The arc is from small to large: a commute
  becomes a flight to orbit, and a stranger met on the Ring becomes the reason to stay or to fight.

## The game, in a sentence

Take off from a real international airport, fly by your own hands to a ring city in orbit, and on the Ring
meet someone: maybe a friend, maybe the person you fall for, maybe the thread that pulls you into
something far larger than your job. The best of those three films, played rather than watched.

## How it meets the canon

- **The flying** is the sketch: from the runway to the Ring and back, by hand, by the flight director, or
  by the crew (the canon's fly-by-intent, §3.5).
- **The people** are Residents, each with their own life (§3.6). The canon's rule holds: the spec never
  names or types the person the Player may come to love; any Resident can become that person.
- **The intrigue**, the Total Recall thread, belongs to the Director (§3.10). It decides what the Player
  is shown and when, from what the Player has done, never by spawning people (L10, nobody is spawned).
  The hidden identity has to be discoverable through Instruments (L5), like everything else.
- **TALLY**, the Player's acknowledged AI (§3.9), is the steady voice through it: it flies when asked,
  reads plans back, and is never the love interest.

## What to keep out

- Names, characters, scenes and designs from the films. They are inspiration only.
- Magic in the flight, except the owner's explicit rule, if it is chosen: the air held to the deck by a
  field (`OPEN_RING_AIR.md`, option 4), and named as a rule.
